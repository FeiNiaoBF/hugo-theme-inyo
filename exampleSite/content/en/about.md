This is the demo site for the Inyo 陰陽 theme. The articles here are not a complete Hugo manual; they show what the theme actually renders, so you can open the page and see the result immediately.

Open source: <https://github.com/FeiNiaoBF/hugo-theme-inyo>

Every design decision is recorded in the theme repo's `DESIGN.md` — each color carries a real name from the 742 traditional Chinese color library and a measured WCAG contrast ratio.

## Demo articles

Six articles ordered as one real usage flow: wire up the theme first, then see how it handles prose, math, and brand ideas.

- [Write an article with Inyo](/posts/theme-usage/): from installing the theme to your first article.
- [Markdown basics](/posts/markdown-basics/): headings, paragraphs, images, code, and tables.
- [Efficient Markdown writing](/posts/markdown-efficient/): summaries, long-form rhythm, task lists, and link boundaries.
- [Math with KaTeX](/posts/katex/): inline formulas, matrices, and aligned equations.
- [FAQ](/posts/faq/): a place to record real questions.
- [Paper, ink, and cinnabar](/posts/brand-design/): Inyo's brand core and trade-offs.
